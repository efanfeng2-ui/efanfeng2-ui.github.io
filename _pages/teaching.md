## Teaching

### ENT 2004 — Insects and Human Society

**Graduate Teaching Assistant**, Virginia Tech · Spring and Fall 2024

A general-education course introducing students to the roles insects play in human society. I supported the asynchronous online course in spring and the in-person course in fall, working with Dr. James Wilson and Dr. Ksenia Onufrieva.

My work included rubric-based grading and feedback in Canvas, preparing short “Insects in the News” presentations, and developing quiz or exam questions from current entomology topics. These assignments gave me practice explaining scientific findings to a broad undergraduate audience.

## Mentoring

### Undergraduate Researchers, Sharakhova Lab

**Research Mentor**, Virginia Tech · 2024 – 2025

I mentored two undergraduate researchers in mosquito molecular biology and cytogenetics. Training included mosquito dissection, FISH probe preparation, and PCR assays for mosquito identification and population studies. I helped troubleshoot COI and ACE assays by reviewing primer dilutions, polymerase choice, reaction composition, cycling conditions, and gel results.

## Outreach

**Hokie BugFest 2025 — Mosquito Genetics exhibit**, Virginia Tech · October 2025. I joined the lab’s public outreach team for an exhibit introducing mosquito genetics through hands-on activities and chromosome models.

I am a member of the [Entomological Society of America](https://www.entsoc.org/) and the Virginia Mosquito Control Association.

## Laboratory Service

I have served as a laboratory safety contact, addressing inspection recommendations and coordinating responses with Virginia Tech Environmental Health and Safety. I also contribute research summaries and FISH figures to NIH grant progress reporting.
